Andreu Matoses Gimenez

I am a PhD candidate in planning for robotics at the Department of Cognitive Robotics (CoR), TU Delft (Netherlands). My research currently focuses on using generative models and physics simulation for task and motion planning problems. I am supervised by Professor Javier Alonso-Mora and Professor Christian Pek.

Before my PhD, I obtained my bachelor's degree in aerospace engineering from Universitat Politècnica de València (Spain), and a master's degree from KTH Royal Institute of Technology (Sweden). During my MSc, I worked as a research engineer at KTH.
